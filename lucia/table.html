The room

Twenty-eight seats,
one long table.

Terrazzo, limewashed plaster, brass, and whatever light comes off the water at seven.

We do not take a booking for the long table. It fills as the evening goes, and most nights it ends up being the best seat in the room.

The room was a chandlery for sixty years and a garage for eleven. We took out the mezzanine, kept the steel windows, and had the plaster mixed with marble dust so it holds the evening light a little longer than it should.

There are two counter seats facing the grill. They are first come, and worth arriving early for.

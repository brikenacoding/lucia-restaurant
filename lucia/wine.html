Wine

Nineteen bottles. Most of the growers Elena has met. Nothing here because a distributor pushed it.

Coastal whites with salt in them, pale reds served cool, and three sweet wines poured at a hundred millilitres.

Every producer, label and region on this list is invented for this project. Glass pours are 150ml; dessert wines 100ml. We keep the reds at sixteen degrees — if yours arrives warm, send it back.

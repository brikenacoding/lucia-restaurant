Our table

We cook over fire
and buy from
fifteen people.

LUCIA occupies a former ship chandlery two streets back from the harbour at Porto Selva.

The floor is the original terrazzo. The long table down the centre seats fourteen, and we seat strangers at it most nights.

There is one grill. It is lit at ten in the morning with olive and holm oak, and by the time the room opens it has burnt down to a bed of coals that will hold until closing. Almost everything passes over it at some point — the octopus, the bread, the whole fish, the lamb, the peppers, sometimes the lemons.

The menu is short because the list of people we buy from is short. Fifteen growers, two fishermen, one miller, and a cheesemaker in the hills above Selvana who will only sell to us on Thursdays. When the boats do not go out, the fish section shrinks and nobody apologises for it. When the first olive oil of the season arrives in November it goes on everything, including the cake.

Sofia Marin cooks. Elena Vesari runs the floor and chose every bottle on the wine list, most of them from growers she has actually met. They opened the room in spring with twenty-eight covers and no intention of adding more.

What arrives, and when

Four lists that decide most of what we cook. Everything else follows from them.

Spring

Green almonds, first artichokes, wild fennel from the cliff path, sea bream.

Summer

Datterini, peaches, courgette flowers, octopus, sardines, the good tomatoes.

Autumn

New olive oil, chestnut honey, mushrooms from inland, the first blue cheese.

Winter

Bitter leaves, blood orange, monkfish, lamb, barley, citrus in everything.

Hospitality

We would
rather know
beforehand.

Tell us about an allergy, a dislike, a child who will only eat pasta, an anniversary you would rather we did not announce. Almost everything on the menu can be adjusted, and it is far easier before the fire than after it.

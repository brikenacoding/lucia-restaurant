Porto Selva
Porto Selva · Modern Mediterranean

One grill, lit at ten. Fifteen growers. Twenty-eight seats, and a long table down the middle.

We took a former ship chandlery two streets back from the harbour, kept the terrazzo floor, and put a wood-fired grill where the counter used to be. Almost everything we serve passes over it — the octopus, the bread, the whole fish, sometimes the lemons.

The menu is short because the list of people we buy from is short. It changes when they tell us it has to. If the boats stayed in, the fish section is thinner that night, and we will say so.

The room

Come at seven,
when the light
turns over.

Limewashed plaster, brass fittings, and whatever comes off the water through the west windows. The long table seats fourteen and we do not take bookings for it — it fills as the evening goes, and most nights it ends up being the best seat in the room.
